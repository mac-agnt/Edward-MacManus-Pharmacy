# Pulse demo script: Edward MacManus Pharmacy

## Start and stop

1. Double-click **Start Demo.command**. Chrome opens at **http://pulse.localhost:8080**.
2. Close that Terminal window when you're done. That stops the demo.

The first time, macOS may ask whether Terminal can access your Downloads folder: click **Allow**.
No internet needed; React and the fonts are bundled in `vendor/`.

## Questions Helios answers

Type these on Home, or click the matching suggestion. Helios listens for the key words.

| Ask | Helios shows | Key words it listens for |
|---|---|---|
| How is the New Life transfer going? | 61 of 142 moved, 81 left, 23 ready, per pharmacy | new life, transfer, blister |
| What shortages are back in stock? | 3 lines back at Uniphar, 27 patients to call | shortage, short, stock, Uniphar |
| Which OTC prices should rise? | 186 lines below median, +€4,850 a month | price, OTC, rise, median |
| How is September against last year? | €512k vs €478k, Finglas down 5.4% | budget, September, last year, turnover |
| When is the accountant pack due? | 30 Sept, 4 of 5 sources in | accountant, pack, Sage, PCRS |
| Create the 27 call tasks | A write tool: drafts it and waits for your yes | call, calls, call list |

Anything else gets: "Everything I reach goes through a registered tool with a declared permission, and none of them covers that question yet." It then offers buttons for the questions above.

## Watch out for

- **Call tasks:** stop at the **NEEDS YOUR YES** card, which is the point to make. **Confirm and create** shows the result.
- Buttons under an answer that start with "Open" jump straight to that page.
